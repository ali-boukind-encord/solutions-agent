CONTEXT
Albert (ex-Invisible, founder of AI Circle, runs annotation workforce) and CTO evaluating Encord to replace Google Sheets for email classification. They need to scale from 15 to 100+ annotators, reduce misclassification errors that cost revenue, and structure 20 different fine-tuning use cases. Pain is acute—growing 0 to $3M ARR in one year.

DEMO FLOW
1. Show 5 HTML emails in Encord (deal opp, newsletter, escalation, phishing, partnership) — highlight how we render docs natively vs their manual sheet copy-paste.
2. Walk the Email Category classification with nested attributes: show how "Important" branches to subcategories (Deal/Revenue, Support, etc) + urgency levels. This replaces their flat spreadsheet structure.
3. Demonstrate "Key Information Extraction" (text-shape annotation) pulling sender intent, action items, deal value, deadlines — show they can tag the actual email content, not just classify.
4. Show "Requires Draft Response" nested logic — if Yes, they tag tone (Formal/Friendly/Concise) and response priority. This feeds their fine-tuning pipeline.
5. Explain Annotator Confidence levels (High/Medium/Low) — filters for QA workflows, reduces Matt's manual validation time.
6. Show ontology reusability: one ontology covers all 20 use cases via classification options. Show export to JSON for their pipeline.
7. Emphasize speed: task setup in minutes vs Sheets iteration cycles, parallel annotation at scale, built-in QA vs manual reviews.

THINGS TO KEEP IN MIND
- Albert knows the space deeply — don't oversell, focus on measurable wins: annotation speed +300%, error rate down, labor scaling.
- Stress structured data extraction from emails (they specifically asked). Show JSON export, highlight how nesting prevents malformed training data.
- This is a live buying decision. Ask about timeline, budget (expect they have it), and decision criteria. Close the conversation, don't just demo.
- Google Sheets comparison: show Encord's collaborative interface, real-time QA queue, and pre-labeling (they don't have this). That's the unlock.
- Keep tone business-minded, not technical. Albert cares about annotation velocity and annotation quality as revenue protection.

INDUSTRY/TECHNICAL CONTEXT
- Email misclassification directly impacts CSM SLA compliance and deal close rates — one lost renewal email = revenue loss. Frame accuracy as revenue insurance.
- Fine-tuning pipelines require high-quality labeled data at scale. Nested taxonomies prevent annotators from creating malformed or inconsistent labels.
- Annotation workforce scaling is non-linear: 15 annotators on Sheets works. 100+ on Sheets breaks (no QA, no consensus, high error variance). Encord handles this.
- Their discontinuation of embeddings was smart — Encord is document-native (HTML, PDF, text, JSON), not a vector db. Position as purpose-built for their stack.
- CSM teams doing email triage need fast iteration cycles. Encord's task reset and ontology version control beat Sheets' manual rework.
